Disclaimer

Draft for review by the project guide · last updated 9 October 2026

This is a student project, not a government website. It is not run by, endorsed by or connected to the Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC), the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board (MPCB) or any other government body. To make an official complaint, use the official channels listed on the map.

Where the information comes from

Hotspots come from our own field visits, each with a date and, where available, a GPS-tagged photo. Everything else (which office is responsible, contact details, project dates) comes from public sources such as government reports, official websites and news reports. Each item shows its source and the date it was last checked.

It may be out of date or wrong

Offices move, phone numbers change, officials are transferred and project dates slip. We re-check entries every 90 days, but anything can change in between. Items marked to verify or to confirm have not been checked with the office yet. Ward and prabhag boundaries on the map are drawn from public boundary files and are approximate.

"Announced" is not "complete"

The sewage plant tracker shows what was promised or reported, and when. We do not show a plant as complete or working until the office, a reply to a Right to Information (RTI) application, or our own site visit confirms it.

What this website does not do

  • It does not file complaints for you. The complaint helper only prepares text; you choose whether to send it, and you are responsible for what you send.
  • It does not give legal advice. Information about RTI applications and complaint routes is general guidance only.
  • It does not take sides in politics. Elected representatives are listed because they represent the area, using official election results, not to praise or blame anyone.
  • It does not test water. Descriptions such as "froth" or "grey water" are what we saw, not laboratory results. Official water-quality data comes from MPCB.

Stay safe

Do not enter the riverbed, the weir or drains to take photos. Take photos from bridges and roads, in daylight, with someone else. Do not photograph people's faces.

Found a mistake?

Please tell us through our GitHub page or through our project guide at the college. Include the hotspot or project ID and, if you can, a source. We correct errors in the sheet first, so the fix reaches every page.
