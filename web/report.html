Report a pollution spot

Seen sewage, foam, dumped waste or a blocked drain on the river? Send a photo. A team member checks every report before it appears on the map.

1. Your photo

Tip: take the photo with the free GPS Map Camera app (search for it in your app store). It prints the exact location and time on the photo, which this page reads for you.

    Downloads about 3 MB the first time.

    2. Hide faces and private details

    Faces are blurred before the photo is sent. Drag on the photo to cover anything else private (a face we missed, a number plate, a house number). Tap a box to switch it off if it covers something that is not private.

      3. Where is it?

      Tap the map or drag the pin to the exact spot. Reports are accepted inside the outlined areas.

        4. What is the problem?

        Please don't write names, phone numbers or emails.

        5. Send

          Thank you. Your report is in.

          A team member will check it. If it is approved, it appears on the map as a resident report (not yet verified by the team). Keep the reference if you want to ask about it.
